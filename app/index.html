Roadmap to the platinum

Difficulty 4/10 · about 200 hours · 1 playthrough · 0 online trophies · free-roam continues after the story, no mission replay · cheats disable trophies and saving.

  1. Step 1 — Finish the story 60–80 h

    Play through all 5 chapters and enjoy it. There are no mission gold medals. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation for Positive Mental Attitude, scan vehicles and study animals as you go (200 vehicles and 100 animals are needed later), and keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — there is no mission replay.

  2. Step 2 — 100% checklist & 70 of 80 challenges ~100 h

    After the story, open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — closer to 60% true completion. Finish the required robberies (39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 8 bank vaults), 100 optional missions, all 42 Shooting Range golds, all 42 drone time trials, 300 stunts and 70 of 80 challenges. Nothing here is missable.

  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks and leftovers 10–20 h

    Finish the 8 qualifying Strangers & Freaks strands and the character questlines (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone), the last meditations, and any miscellaneous trophies still open. Then the platinum, Vice Got a Price!, is yours.
