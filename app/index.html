Platinum: Vice Got a Price! — pops when the other 54 are done. Difficulty 4/10 · about 200 hours · 5 chapters · no mission replay · cheats disable trophies and saving.

Roadmap to the platinum

  1. Step 1 — Play the story (60–80 h). Chapters 1–5, all story trophies. Do the one possibly missable thing in Chapter 1 (see the warning below) and pick up optional missions as they appear — they count toward Step 2. Free-roam continues after the story.
  2. Step 2 — The 100% checklist (~100 h). Work the Progress > Stats > 100% Completion list: 100 optional missions, 70 of 80 challenges, 200 of 277 base-model vehicles scanned, 100 of 255 animals studied, 300 of 397 stunts, all 42 Shooting Range golds, all 42 drone time trials, 30 race wins, 8 bank jobs, all 39 gang operations, 15 of 18 card sets. Nothing here is missable.
  3. Step 3 — Strangers & Freaks leftovers (10–20 h). Finish the 8 qualifying stranger strands and any questlines still open (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone, Bret). All doable in free-roam after the story.

⚠ The only possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. Meditate at Jason's house in Chapter 1 — after “Down to Earth”, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and choose “Accept”. It is unconfirmed whether that first meditation counts toward the trophy, so do it in Chapter 1 to be safe. Every other trophy can be earned after the story.
